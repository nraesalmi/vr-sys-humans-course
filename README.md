# Bandit Shooter VR

### Demo video:

[![Final project Demo](https://img.youtube.com/vi/ooPvudixFrM/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ooPvudixFrM)

**Bandit Shooter VR** is a wave-based virtual reality shooter where you defend a small-town bank from relentless bandits. Built for mobile Android VR, the game focuses on immersive motion-based controls, physical reloading mechanics, and escalating difficulty. The game was made as the final project for the VR Systems and Humans -course.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Defend the bank from waves of bandits trying to rob the town  
- Each wave spawns enemies with different health, speed, and damage  
- If bandits reach the bank, money is deducted from the cash pool  
- The game ends when the bank runs out of money  
- Shoot all enemies in a wave to progress to the next one  

Shoot the target at the start of the game when you are ready to begin.

---

## 🕹 Controls

### Camera
- **Look around:** Tilt your phone while in VR mode  

### Shooting
- **Shoot:** Tap the phone screen  

### Reloading (when out of ammo)
1. Tilt phone **left** to open the gun chamber  
2. Tilt phone **up** to eject empty casings  
3. Tilt phone **down**, then tilt **right** to close the chamber  

The reload process is fully physical and requires completing each step in order.
